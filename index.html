HOW TO PLAY

Computer

  • W A S D walk, Shift run
  • Mouse to look around
  • E or click to pick up a cat
  • Esc or P to pause

Phone

  • Left circle to walk
  • Drag the right side to look around
  • PICK UP to grab a cat
  • Plays best in landscape

Controller

  • Left stick to walk (click it or hold RT to run)
  • Right stick to look
  • A / ✕ to pick up a cat
  • Start pause · B / ◯ back

Photo mode

  • Choose PHOTO MODE in the pause menu
  • Fly with W A S D, R up, F down
  • Enter to take a photo
  • F3 anytime: FPS and position

Tips

  • Cats' eyes shine when your flashlight hits them.
  • Listen for meows; they come from the left or right.
  • Look under, behind and on top of things.
  • In NIGHTMARE: keep your light on it.
